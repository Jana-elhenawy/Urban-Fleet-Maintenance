# Urban Fleet & Maintenance Hub

A comprehensive Fleet Management System designed to help transportation agencies and organizations manage vehicles, maintenance schedules, inspections, drivers, and operational records efficiently.

## 📋 Overview

Urban Fleet & Maintenance Hub is a database-driven application that centralizes fleet operations and maintenance management. The system enables administrators to track vehicles, schedule maintenance activities, monitor inspections, manage drivers, and generate operational reports.

This project was developed as part of a Database Systems project focusing on real-world fleet management challenges.

---

## 🚀 Features

### Vehicle Management
- Add, update, and delete vehicles
- Store vehicle specifications
- Track vehicle status and availability
- Categorize vehicles by model and fuel type

### Maintenance Management
- Schedule preventive maintenance
- Record maintenance history
- Track maintenance costs
- Monitor upcoming maintenance requirements

### Driver Management
- Register and manage drivers
- Assign drivers to vehicles
- Maintain driver records and contact information

### Inspection Tracking
- Record vehicle inspections
- Monitor inspection status
- Store inspection history
- Generate inspection reports

### Work Orders
- Create maintenance work orders
- Assign technicians
- Track work order progress
- Record completed maintenance activities

### Reporting
- Vehicle utilization reports
- Maintenance history reports
- Inspection reports
- Fleet performance analytics
